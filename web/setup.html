pulseSSS Phone (iPhone app)
Your iPhone holds the Air and sends your heart rate, also in the background.

Install (once)

  1. Run AltServer on this PC as administrator (tray icon, bottom right).
  2. Plug the iPhone in with a cable and unlock it. Tray icon → Install AltStore → your iPhone, sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust. Then Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode on (the phone restarts).
  4. AltStore → Sources → + → paste this, then install pulseSSS Phone (keep the cable in):

Pair (once)

  1. Click Pair phone here and scan the code with the iPhone camera. Tap the banner; the app says "paired with forbandelse.com". Allow Bluetooth.

Leaving the PC

  1. Click Use phone here (this also lets go of the Air).
  2. Open pulseSSS Phone. With Share heart rate on in Google Health, it connects within about 30 seconds. You can lock the phone; it keeps sending. Swiping the app away stops it.

Back at the PC

  1. In the app, tap release the air.
  2. Here, click Connect Fitbit Air.

Every week

  1. Free Apple IDs make the app expire after 7 days. Keep AltServer running and AltStore re-signs it by itself; if it can't find the PC over Wi-Fi, plug the phone in for a moment (or turn on Sync with this iPhone over Wi-Fi in iTunes so it works without the cable).
  2. New versions of the app appear as updates in AltStore.
  1. Install pulseSSS Phone on your iPhone (see the README).
  2. Scan this code with the iPhone camera and open it in pulseSSS Phone.
  3. Press Use phone here, then connect the Air in the app.

Anyone with this code can post heart rate to your site. Unpair makes it stop working.

Pulsoid
Reads your heart rate from the Pulsoid app on your phone.

Pulsoid token

Fitbit Air: on your phone, Google Health → Connections → Share heart rate. Each new connection takes up to 30 seconds while the Air gets ready, so pulseSSS stays connected in the background.

Pulsoid: the Air connects to only one device at a time, so press Use Pulsoid here first (that frees it), then connect the Air in the Pulsoid app. The token comes from pulsoid.net/ui/keys (a Pulsoid BRO feature; the free trial works). forbandelse.com also uses it to show Pulsoid while this PC is off.

OBS

  1. In OBS, open Tools → Scripts, click + and choose obs\pulsesss.lua in the pulseSSS folder.
  2. Click Add overlay to current scene in the script panel.
  3. Optional: edit designs inside OBS with Docks → Custom Browser Docks and the URL http://127.0.0.1:8765/design.
